Model scope: closed-loop batch simulation, single quad (N=1), corrected termination (both end membranes CEM — D2 at the cathode end, C1 at the anode end) to keep metal cations out of the cathode rinse and Cl⁻ out of the anode rinse. Ion transport at each membrane is split between competing same-sign species by a conductivity-weighted share (Kohlrausch's law, standard limiting molar conductivities) — not a fixed efficiency constant. Electrode reactions (H2/OH⁻ at the cathode rinse, O2/H+ at the anode rinse) are modeled as Faradaic source terms. Voltage is derived from a simplified compartment + membrane resistance chain (spacer gap 0.5 cm, membrane areal resistance 5 Ω·cm² each, fixed 1.5 V electrode overpotential) — all flagged placeholders, not measured values. pH is a simplified proxy (H⁺/OH⁻ tracked as trace migrating species plus electrode generation, water-equilibrium clamp for display) — not a rigorous aqueous-equilibrium solver.
